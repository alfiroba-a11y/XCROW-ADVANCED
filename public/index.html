HOW XCROW WORKS
01

Agree on the deal

Set the transaction title, amount, fee arrangement, payment method, and inspection window before funding.

02

Join one shared room

Invite the buyer and seller with a private escrow code. Participants can follow the same deal status and conversation.

03

Track the outcome

Follow payment and delivery milestones. Finished escrows show whether funds were released or refunded in the participants’ history.

XCROW is designed for freelancers, buyers, and sellers handling local or cross-border online deals. KES and USDT payment options are shown with the applicable escrow instructions. Review the terms and payment details before sending funds.
